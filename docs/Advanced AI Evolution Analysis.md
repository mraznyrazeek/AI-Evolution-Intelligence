# Advanced AI Evolution Analysis

## 1. Historical Evolution Analysis

### 1.1 Historical Summary

The historical analysis evaluated the AI Evolution Index across 264 technology-month observations covering six AI technology categories from January 2023 to August 2026.

The long-term average scores were:

| Technology | Average Score | Median Score | Minimum | Maximum | Volatility | Average Momentum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| LLM | 87.08 | 87.47 | 51.27 | 99.65 | 9.67 | 0.30 |
| AI Agents | 59.17 | 59.44 | 40.05 | 73.42 | 8.03 | 0.25 |
| RAG | 52.82 | 49.12 | 35.63 | 79.94 | 10.61 | 0.06 |
| MCP | 48.33 | 49.32 | 31.38 | 66.36 | 9.44 | -0.67 |
| Multimodal AI | 29.79 | 32.71 | 0.30 | 45.61 | 12.48 | 2.19 |
| Reasoning AI | 24.74 | 32.54 | 0.30 | 47.00 | 18.32 | 2.57 |

LLM maintained the highest average AI Evolution Score throughout the study period. AI Agents formed the second-highest long-term category, while RAG and MCP occupied the middle range.

Reasoning AI showed the highest volatility, followed by Multimodal AI. This indicates greater variation in their measured evolution scores over time.

### 1.2 Start-to-End Evolution

The change between the first available month (2023-01) and the latest month (2026-08) was:

| Technology | Start Score | End Score | Absolute Change | Percentage Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Multimodal AI | 2.10 | 38.94 | +36.84 | +1751.29% |
| Reasoning AI | 13.53 | 35.13 | +21.60 | +159.71% |
| LLM | 51.27 | 66.73 | +15.46 | +30.15% |
| AI Agents | 40.05 | 49.52 | +9.48 | +23.67% |
| RAG | 35.63 | 39.08 | +3.45 | +9.68% |
| MCP | 46.78 | 39.01 | -7.77 | -16.62% |

Multimodal AI and Reasoning AI recorded the largest absolute increases in their composite scores. MCP was the only technology whose end score was lower than its starting score.

The very large percentage increase for Multimodal AI should be interpreted cautiously because its starting score was close to the lower normalization boundary. Therefore, absolute score change provides a more meaningful comparison than percentage growth alone for low-baseline technologies.

### 1.3 Peak Analysis

The highest recorded score for each technology occurred at the following points:

| Technology | Peak Month | Peak Score |
|---|---|---:|

The timing of peak scores differs substantially between technologies. LLM reached its maximum relatively early in the observed period, whereas Multimodal AI and Reasoning AI reached their highest recorded scores later.

These peak dates provide potential evidence of different evolutionary trajectories and will be investigated further through turning-point and acceleration/deceleration analysis.

### 1.7 Rank Crossovers

The historical analysis identified multiple changes in relative technology rankings.

Important examples include:

- AI Agents and MCP crossed positions multiple times.
- AI Agents and RAG also experienced several ranking changes.
- MCP and Multimodal AI crossed positions during 2024–2025.
- MCP and RAG crossed positions multiple times.
- Multimodal AI and Reasoning AI experienced numerous rank changes.
- Multimodal AI moved above RAG during May 2026 and remained above it through August 2026.

These crossovers indicate that technology rankings are not completely static, even though LLM remained dominant overall.

### 1.8 Recent Momentum

The latest six-month momentum values were:

| Technology | Recent Momentum |
|---|---:|
| Multimodal AI | -0.66 |
| RAG | -1.63 |
| Reasoning AI | -1.73 |
| LLM | -2.18 |
| MCP | -2.38 |
| AI Agents | -2.85 |

All six technologies recorded negative recent momentum.

This does not by itself demonstrate that the technologies are declining in real-world importance. The measure represents recent changes in the normalized AI Evolution Index and may reflect short-term ecosystem fluctuations.

The recent momentum results should therefore be combined with longer-term trends, source-level signals, and subsequent acceleration/deceleration analysis.

### 1.9 Directional Consistency

The proportion of months showing positive month-to-month score changes was:

| Technology | Positive Months | Negative Months | Positive Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Reasoning AI | 25 | 15 | 58.14% |
| Multimodal AI | 24 | 19 | 55.81% |
| AI Agents | 22 | 21 | 51.16% |
| LLM | 20 | 23 | 46.51% |
| MCP | 19 | 24 | 44.19% |
| RAG | 17 | 26 | 39.53% |

Reasoning AI and Multimodal AI had the highest proportion of positive monthly changes, whereas RAG had the lowest.

This suggests that some lower-ranked technologies experienced more frequent upward movements despite having lower absolute index scores.

### 1.10 Historical Analysis Conclusion

The historical analysis demonstrates that the six AI technology categories have followed different evolutionary patterns.

LLM maintained clear long-term dominance, while AI Agents remained the second-highest category on average. RAG and MCP occupied the middle range but experienced repeated ranking changes. Multimodal AI and Reasoning AI recorded substantial long-term increases but also showed higher volatility.

The results provide a descriptive foundation for the next stage of the research. However, the observed movements cannot yet be interpreted as sustained growth, decline, or technology lifecycle transitions.

Therefore, the next analysis will investigate **acceleration, deceleration, and turning points** to determine whether observed changes represent temporary fluctuations or more persistent evolutionary patterns.
