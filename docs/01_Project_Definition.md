# Project Definition

**Project Title:** Machine Learning-Based AI Technology Evolution Analysis and Future Trend Prediction Platform

**Document Version:** 1.0

**Date:** 16 August 2026

**Status:** Initial Project Definition

## 1. Project Overview

This project proposes the development of a machine learning-based platform for analysing and understanding the evolution of modern artificial intelligence technologies and models over time.

The system will collect and analyse information from relevant public data sources, including developer communities, open-source ecosystems, AI model releases, and other suitable technology-related sources. The collected data will be used to identify changes in AI technologies, emerging trends, capability shifts, technology growth and decline, and relationships between different AI technologies.

Machine learning, natural language processing, statistical analysis, and time-series techniques will be used to analyse historical patterns and estimate potential future trajectories of AI technologies. The system will also aim to identify emerging technologies, detect significant changes in the AI ecosystem, and determine which technologies or capabilities show stronger future development potential.

The results will be presented through a web-based interactive platform that allows users to explore AI technology evolution, technology lifecycles, emerging trends, historical patterns, future predictions, and relationships between AI technologies.

## 2. Background

Artificial intelligence has experienced rapid development in recent years, with significant advances in large language models, generative AI, multimodal systems, reasoning models, AI agents, retrieval-augmented generation, and other emerging technologies. New models, frameworks, tools, and capabilities are introduced frequently, while existing technologies continue to improve and change.

The rapid development of the AI ecosystem makes it increasingly difficult to understand how individual AI models and technologies are evolving over time. A technology that is emerging today may become widely adopted in the future, while another technology may reach maturity or decline as new approaches become available. In addition, the evolution of AI is not limited to improvements in model performance. It also includes changes in capabilities, developer adoption, supporting technologies, open-source activity, community interest, and the relationships between different technologies.

Large amounts of information related to AI development are generated through online developer communities, open-source repositories, model releases, technical discussions, and other publicly available sources. These sources provide valuable historical signals that can be analysed to understand changes within the AI ecosystem. However, this information is distributed across different platforms and is not normally presented as a unified view of technological evolution.

Data analysis, natural language processing, and machine learning techniques provide opportunities to process these large and diverse datasets. By analysing historical patterns, technology relationships, developer activity, and other measurable indicators, it may be possible to identify emerging trends, detect significant changes, measure technology evolution, and estimate potential future trajectories.

Therefore, this project focuses on developing an integrated platform for analysing the evolution of AI technologies and models. Rather than providing only a current ranking of AI tools, the proposed system aims to examine how the AI ecosystem changes over time and use historical evidence to identify potential future directions.

## 3. Problem Statement

The rapid development of artificial intelligence has resulted in a continuously changing ecosystem of AI models, technologies, frameworks, and capabilities. New technologies are introduced frequently, existing models are updated, and the focus of AI development shifts between different capabilities such as text generation, coding, reasoning, multimodal processing, retrieval-augmented generation, and autonomous AI agents.

Although large amounts of AI-related information are available through developer communities, open-source repositories, model releases, technical discussions, benchmark results, and other public sources, this information is fragmented across different platforms. Existing AI comparison and ranking platforms generally focus on current model capabilities, benchmark scores, or individual technology trends. These approaches provide limited insight into how AI technologies have evolved over time, why certain technologies are gaining momentum, which technologies are declining, and what future directions may emerge.

Furthermore, the rapid pace of AI development makes manual analysis difficult and time-consuming. Identifying long-term trends, relationships between technologies, emerging capabilities, and significant changes within the AI ecosystem requires the analysis of large amounts of historical and continuously changing data.

Therefore, there is a need for an integrated data-driven system that can collect and analyse AI ecosystem data, identify patterns of technological evolution, detect emerging and declining technologies, measure their development using meaningful indicators, and apply machine learning techniques to estimate potential future trajectories. The proposed system aims to address this gap by providing an interactive platform for analysing and understanding the evolution of modern AI technologies.

## 4. Project Aim

The aim of this project is to develop a machine learning-based web platform that analyses the historical evolution of modern artificial intelligence technologies and models, identifies emerging trends, capability shifts, technology growth and decline, and uses historical patterns to estimate potential future trajectories within the AI ecosystem.

## 5. Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are:

1. **To investigate and identify suitable public data sources** containing information related to modern AI models, technologies, capabilities, developer activity, and ecosystem development.

2. **To design and implement a data collection pipeline** capable of gathering and storing relevant historical and continuously updated AI-related data from selected sources.

3. **To clean, preprocess, and normalise heterogeneous AI-related data** to create structured datasets suitable for data analysis, natural language processing, and machine learning.

4. **To analyse the historical evolution of AI technologies and models** by examining changes in technology activity, capabilities, adoption, relationships, and other relevant indicators over time.

5. **To apply natural language processing and data mining techniques** to identify AI technologies, topics, relationships, sentiment, emerging trends, and significant changes within AI-related discussions.

6. **To develop an AI Evolution Index and technology lifecycle framework** for measuring and categorising the development, momentum, growth, maturity, and decline of AI technologies.

7. **To develop and evaluate machine learning and time-series models** for identifying emerging technologies and estimating potential future trajectories and changes within the AI ecosystem.

8. **To develop an interactive web-based platform** that presents AI evolution analysis, technology lifecycles, emerging trends, predictions, relationships, and analytical insights in an accessible and understandable manner.

## 6. Research Questions

The project will investigate the following research questions:

1. **How have modern AI models, technologies, and capabilities evolved over time?**

2. **What measurable factors and ecosystem signals are associated with the growth, maturity, or decline of AI technologies?**

3. **Can natural language processing and data mining techniques be used to identify emerging AI technologies, trends, capability shifts, and relationships from large-scale AI-related data?**

4. **Can an AI Evolution Index provide a meaningful representation of the development and momentum of different AI technologies?**

5. **To what extent can machine learning and time-series models use historical AI ecosystem data to estimate future technology trajectories?**

6. **Which AI technologies and capabilities show the strongest potential for future development based on their historical evolution patterns and observed ecosystem signals?**

## 7. Project Scope

### 7.1 In Scope

The project will focus on the analysis of modern AI models, technologies, capabilities, and their development over time. The system will investigate suitable publicly available data sources and integrate relevant information to support AI evolution analysis and prediction.

The project will include:

- Collection of historical and current data related to modern AI models and technologies from selected public data sources.
- Analysis of developer and community activity related to AI technologies.
- Analysis of AI model and technology releases and development where suitable data is available.
- Identification and analysis of AI capabilities such as coding, reasoning, multimodal processing, retrieval-augmented generation, tool use, and AI agents.
- Data cleaning, preprocessing, and normalisation of collected information.
- Exploratory analysis of AI technology growth, decline, adoption, and development patterns.
- Natural language processing for technology identification, topic detection, sentiment analysis, and relationship discovery from textual data.
- Detection of emerging and declining AI technologies.
- Development of an AI Evolution Index to represent the development and momentum of selected AI technologies.
- Development of a technology lifecycle framework to classify technologies according to their observed stage of development.
- Application of machine learning and time-series techniques to estimate future AI technology trajectories.
- Detection of significant or unusual changes within AI technology activity.
- Development of an interactive web-based platform for presenting the analysis and predictions.
- Visualisation of historical trends, technology relationships, lifecycle stages, predictions, and analytical results.
- Evaluation of the data-processing pipeline, analytical methods, machine learning models, and web application.

### 7.2 Out of Scope

The project will not attempt to:

- Develop or train a new large language model or foundation model.
- Reproduce proprietary AI models or their internal architectures.
- Determine an objectively universal "best" AI model for all tasks.
- Predict the exact future release dates, features, or decisions of AI companies.
- Guarantee which AI company or model will dominate the future AI market.
- Predict financial performance, stock prices, or commercial revenue of AI companies.
- Replace established AI benchmark organisations or independently certify AI model performance.
- Collect private, restricted, or personally identifiable user information.
- Treat predictions generated by the system as certain future outcomes.
- Analyse every AI technology or AI model available worldwide.
- Guarantee complete coverage of all AI-related online activity.

## 8. Key Concepts

The following concepts define the main areas of analysis within the proposed system.

### 8.1 AI Model

An AI model refers to a computational model designed to perform one or more artificial intelligence tasks. In this project, the term may include modern foundation models and generative AI models such as large language models and multimodal models.

### 8.2 AI Technology

An AI technology refers to a broader technological approach, framework, capability, or method used to develop or operate AI systems. Examples may include retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), AI agents, tool calling, multimodal AI, model orchestration, and other emerging technologies.

### 8.3 AI Capability

An AI capability refers to a type of task or functionality that an AI system can perform. Examples include text generation, coding, reasoning, mathematical problem solving, image understanding, tool use, retrieval, and autonomous task execution.

### 8.4 AI Technology Evolution

AI technology evolution refers to the changes and development of AI models, technologies, and capabilities over time. In this project, evolution may be observed through changes in technology activity, adoption signals, capabilities, relationships, ecosystem development, and other measurable indicators.

### 8.5 AI Evolution Index

The AI Evolution Index is a proposed composite analytical measure that represents the relative development and momentum of a selected AI technology based on multiple measurable indicators. The exact indicators and weighting methodology will be determined following data-source investigation and exploratory analysis.

### 8.6 Technology Lifecycle

The technology lifecycle represents the development stage of an AI technology based on observed patterns of activity and evolution. The proposed lifecycle may include stages such as experimental, emerging, growing, mainstream, mature, and declining.

### 8.7 Emerging Technology

An emerging technology refers to an AI technology that demonstrates increasing activity, adoption, discussion, ecosystem development, or other signals indicating potential future significance.

### 8.8 Technology Trajectory

A technology trajectory represents the historical and projected direction of an AI technology based on changes in its measurable indicators over time. The trajectory may indicate growth, stability, decline, or changing development patterns.

### 8.9 AI Ecosystem

The AI ecosystem refers to the interconnected collection of AI models, technologies, developers, open-source projects, communities, platforms, and supporting technologies involved in the development and use of artificial intelligence.

### 8.10 AI Ecosystem Disruption

An AI ecosystem disruption refers to a significant or unusual change in observed AI-related activity or development patterns. Such changes may be associated with major model releases, technological breakthroughs, newly emerging technologies, or substantial changes in developer and community activity.

## 9. Expected System Outputs

The proposed system is expected to provide the following outputs:

### 9.1 AI Technology Evolution Analysis

The system will provide historical analysis showing how selected AI models, technologies, and capabilities have changed over time. Users will be able to examine changes in activity, development, adoption signals, and other relevant indicators.

### 9.2 AI Evolution Timeline

The system will provide visual timelines showing significant developments, model releases, technology growth, and major changes associated with selected AI technologies.

### 9.3 Technology Growth and Decline Analysis

The system will identify technologies demonstrating increasing, stable, or decreasing activity and provide visual comparisons of their historical trajectories.

### 9.4 Emerging Technology Detection

The system will identify AI technologies and capabilities that demonstrate significant growth or other signals associated with emerging importance.

### 9.5 Technology Lifecycle Classification

The system will classify selected AI technologies according to their observed lifecycle stage, such as experimental, emerging, growing, mainstream, mature, or declining.

### 9.6 AI Evolution Index

The system will calculate an AI Evolution Index for selected technologies using a combination of measurable indicators derived from the collected data. The index will provide a comparative representation of technology development and momentum.

### 9.7 Future Trajectory Prediction

Machine learning and time-series models will be used to estimate potential future trajectories for selected AI technologies and capabilities based on historical patterns.

### 9.8 Potential Future Leaders

The system will identify AI technologies or capabilities that demonstrate stronger predicted future trajectories relative to other technologies within the selected analysis scope.

### 9.9 AI Ecosystem Disruption Detection

The system will identify significant or unusual changes in AI-related activity and provide information about the technologies, capabilities, or ecosystem areas associated with the detected changes.

### 9.10 AI Technology Relationship Graph

The system will provide an interactive visual representation of relationships between AI models, technologies, capabilities, topics, and related ecosystem components.

### 9.11 Explainable Predictions

Where machine learning models are used for predictions, the system will provide information about the major factors contributing to the predictions using appropriate model interpretation techniques.

### 9.12 Interactive Web Dashboard

All major analytical results will be presented through an interactive web-based dashboard that allows users to explore AI evolution, trends, technology lifecycles, predictions, relationships, and other system outputs.

## 10. High-Level System Workflow

The proposed system will follow a multi-stage workflow that transforms raw AI ecosystem data into analytical insights and future predictions.

### Stage 1 — Data Source Investigation

Potential public data sources will be investigated to identify sources that provide reliable and relevant information about AI models, technologies, capabilities, developer activity, open-source development, model releases, and related ecosystem signals.

### Stage 2 — Data Collection

Data will be collected from the selected sources using appropriate APIs, public datasets, or other permitted data-access methods. The collection process will capture both historical information and, where feasible, newly available data.

### Stage 3 — Data Storage

Collected data will be stored in a structured data storage system. A database will be considered for managing the collected information and supporting efficient retrieval and analysis.

### Stage 4 — Data Cleaning and Normalisation

The collected data will be processed to remove duplicates, handle missing or invalid values, standardise dates and terminology, remove irrelevant information, and ensure consistency across different data sources.

### Stage 5 — Exploratory Data Analysis

Statistical and visual analysis will be performed to understand the distribution, growth, decline, relationships, and temporal patterns within the collected AI ecosystem data.

### Stage 6 — Natural Language Processing

Textual data will be analysed using natural language processing techniques to identify AI technologies, models, capabilities, topics, sentiment, relationships, and other relevant information contained within AI-related discussions.

### Stage 7 — Evolution Analysis

Historical indicators will be analysed to determine how AI models, technologies, and capabilities have developed over time. Growth patterns, declining trends, technology relationships, and significant changes will be identified.

### Stage 8 — Feature Engineering and Evolution Index

Relevant analytical indicators will be transformed into machine learning features. A proposed AI Evolution Index will be developed to represent the relative development and momentum of selected technologies.

### Stage 9 — Machine Learning and Prediction

Machine learning and time-series techniques will be applied to historical data to identify emerging technologies, estimate future technology trajectories, and investigate potential changes in the AI ecosystem.

### Stage 10 — Disruption and Emerging Trend Detection

Statistical and machine learning techniques will be used to identify unusual changes and accelerating trends that may indicate significant developments or emerging technologies.

### Stage 11 — Results and Visualisation

The analytical results will be transformed into interactive visualisations, including timelines, trend charts, lifecycle indicators, rankings, relationship graphs, and prediction displays.

### Stage 12 — Web Application

The final results will be integrated into an interactive web-based platform. Users will be able to explore AI technology evolution, emerging trends, lifecycle stages, predicted trajectories, ecosystem relationships, and analytical explanations.

### Overall Workflow

The overall process can be summarised as:

**Data Sources → Data Collection → Data Storage → Data Cleaning → Exploratory Analysis → NLP → Evolution Analysis → Feature Engineering → AI Evolution Index → Machine Learning → Trend and Disruption Detection → Visualisation → Web Application**

## 11. Project Novelty and Contribution

The proposed project aims to provide a broader view of artificial intelligence development by focusing on the evolution of AI technologies rather than only comparing their current performance or popularity.

The main novelty of the project is the integration of multiple forms of AI ecosystem data to analyse how technologies change over time. Instead of treating AI models and technologies as isolated entities, the system will investigate their development, relationships, adoption signals, capability changes, and lifecycle patterns.

The project will provide several distinctive analytical components:

- **AI Evolution Analysis:** Examining how AI models, technologies, and capabilities change over time rather than focusing only on their current state.
- **AI Evolution Index:** Developing a composite measure to represent the relative development and momentum of selected AI technologies using multiple measurable indicators.
- **Technology Lifecycle Analysis:** Classifying technologies according to their observed development stages, from emerging technologies to mature or declining technologies.
- **Emerging Trend Detection:** Identifying technologies and capabilities that demonstrate accelerating activity or other signals of potential future importance.
- **AI Ecosystem Disruption Detection:** Identifying significant changes or unusual patterns that may indicate major developments within the AI ecosystem.
- **Technology Relationship Analysis:** Examining relationships between AI models, technologies, capabilities, topics, and supporting technologies.
- **Future Trajectory Prediction:** Applying machine learning and time-series techniques to estimate potential future development trajectories based on historical patterns.
- **Explainable AI Insights:** Providing information about the factors that contribute to predictions and analytical results rather than presenting predictions as unexplained outputs.

The project therefore aims to move beyond a conventional AI comparison or ranking system by combining historical analysis, natural language processing, machine learning, trend detection, lifecycle analysis, and interactive visualisation within a single platform.

The contribution of the project will be a data-driven framework for studying the evolution of the AI ecosystem and providing evidence-based insights into potential future technology directions. The predictions produced by the system will be treated as estimates based on observed historical patterns rather than guaranteed future outcomes.

## 14. Project Limitations and Assumptions

### 14.1 Limitations

The project is expected to have several limitations:

- The availability and accessibility of historical data may vary between data sources.
- Some platforms may impose API restrictions, rate limits, authentication requirements, or usage conditions.
- Public online data may contain noise, duplicate information, incomplete records, spam, or irrelevant content.
- Developer and community activity may not represent the entire global AI ecosystem.
- Online discussion activity does not necessarily represent actual commercial adoption or technical capability.
- AI technologies develop rapidly, meaning predictions may become less reliable when significant unexpected technological developments occur.
- Different benchmark systems may use different evaluation methodologies and may not be directly comparable.
- Newly emerging technologies may have insufficient historical data for reliable machine learning predictions.
- The proposed AI Evolution Index will depend on the selected indicators and methodology and therefore should be interpreted as an analytical measure rather than an absolute measurement of technological progress.
- Machine learning predictions will represent estimates based on historical patterns and available data rather than guaranteed future outcomes.

### 14.2 Assumptions

The project will operate under the following assumptions:

- Relevant public AI-related data can be obtained through permitted access methods.
- The selected data sources provide sufficient historical information for meaningful analysis.
- The collected data contains measurable signals related to AI technology development and evolution.
- Historical patterns contain useful information for identifying trends and estimating future trajectories.
- Data preprocessing and normalisation can reduce major inconsistencies between different sources.
- Selected machine learning models can identify meaningful relationships within the prepared datasets.
- AI technology evolution can be represented using a combination of measurable indicators rather than a single variable.
- The selected AI models, technologies, and capabilities will provide sufficient coverage to demonstrate the proposed methodology.

### 14.3 Scope Control

The project will prioritise the quality and analytical value of the selected data sources over the number of sources included. Additional data sources, AI models, or technologies may be excluded if they do not provide sufficient historical coverage, reliable data, or practical access.

The final system architecture, data sources, technology list, features, and machine learning methodology may therefore be refined following the data-source investigation and exploratory analysis stages.
